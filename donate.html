Why birds need your help

📉

3 Billion Lost

North America has lost nearly 3 billion birds since 1970 — almost 30% of all bird populations — due to habitat loss, pesticides, and climate change.

🌲

Habitat at Risk

Longleaf pine forests — home to the endangered Red-Cockaded Woodpecker — have been reduced to less than 3% of their original range.

🌊

Coastal Threats

Brunswick County's coastal bird habitats face threats from development, sea level rise, and human disturbance of nesting areas.

📚

Education Matters

Communities that understand birds protect them. Free education is the most powerful conservation tool we have.
